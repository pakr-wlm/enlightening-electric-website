Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take emergency calls at night and on weekends?

Do you work on both homes and businesses?

Yes. We do residential and commercial electrical work, including commercial lighting and new construction.

Do you install standby generators?

Yes. Standby generator installation is one of our services.

What is structured cabling?

It's the low-voltage cabling behind an office's phones and computer network. We install it alongside standard electrical work.

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Common signs are breakers that trip often, a panel that is very old, and lights that dim when appliances start. An electrician has to look at it in person to say for sure.

Is it safe to keep using a breaker that keeps tripping?

Not for long. A breaker trips to protect the wiring from overload or a fault. If it keeps happening, turn off what's on that circuit and have it checked.
